Information for Participants


We may play the following classics if we run out of ideas at the jam session:
  • Jazzy Tunes:
  • Autumn Leaves (G-)
  • Bernie's Tune
  • Body And Soul (Db)
  • Bye Bye Blackbird (F)
  • Days Of Wine And Roses (F)
  • Doxy
  • Fly Me To The Moon (C)
  • Freddie Freeloader (Bb)
  • How High The Moon (G)
  • Mr. Magic (C-)
  • On Green Dolphin Street (Eb)
  • Stella By Starlight (Bb)
  • There Will Never Be Another You (Eb)
  • Funky/Rock Tunes:
  • Chameleon 1 (Bb-)
  • Hey Joe
  • Honky Tonk Woman
  • I Can’t Get No Satisfaction
  • Memphis Underground (C)
  • Red Baron (G-)
  • Smoke On the Water
  • Sunshine of Your Love

We follow the usual jam session custom of max 2 songs per performer until all performers in the room have been called on stage, with an exception for rhythm section members who can stay longer on stage if nobody can take up their role.
